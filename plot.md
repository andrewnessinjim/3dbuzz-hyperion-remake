# Plot

You are part of the Sagewood tribe that's lived in the Lushglade forest for thousands of years. For the past few years, your tribe's only source of food, the Sagewood tree, are producing less and less fruits. Some of them have even begun to die. Your tribe has cultivated Sagewood trees as long as anybody can remember and they cannot leave the trees unprotected because the tribe cannot afford fruits being stolen by other tribes or animals. While a part of your tribe is exploring new fruits, it doesn't look promising, especially because the tribe can't move too far from the Sagewood trees. Every attempt in the past to consume other fruits have resulted in infections and deaths. You are the only person in the tribe who is able to eat other fruits and no one knows why. The tribe leader has sent you to explore deeper parts of the forest to find out what's affecting the Sagewood trees.

You begin your journey from the Sagewood Groove. The trees with reduced yield have yellow patches on their leaves.

Dead pieces of Cyder tree barks with yellow patterns on them.

An owl who is able to hear what the trees' thoughts and whose thoughts you can hear.

Trees are able to infect each other and choke each others' roots.

Sagewood Trees: want to survive, manipulating the Sagewood tribe
Gypsy Trees: new species on the rise. manipulating another tribe.

Player: want to help the tribe survive
Sagewood Owl: wants the Sagewood trees to thrive, so it can make its nests in it

Sagewood trees near the pool of Mira have no infection.

### Endings

Give enough narrative to make the decision between 1 and 2 a difficult one. For each ending, the player learns the tree's manipulation technique.

#### Ending 1: Save Sagewood Tribe, Kill Duskbloom Tribe
Trigger: bring the water sample from the pool of Mira to the Tribe leader

#### Ending 2: Save Duskbloom Tribe, Kill Sagewood Tribe
Trigger: Poison the pool of Mira so Sagewood loses what remains of its strength

#### Ending 3: Both Sagewood and Duskbloom fight each other to the death.
Trigger: After a certain number of moves without ending 1 or 2


## World Rules
1. Trees can communicate among themselves through leaves, roots or barks and its the tree that decides which one to use for which message.
2. Barks can be interpreted by Featherbough owls.
3. Roots can be interpreted by Rootle Voles.
4. Duskbloom is immune to Nyra.
5. Nyra can poison Mira.
6. Sagewood has manipulated the Sagewood tribe.
7. Duskbloom has not manipulated anyone.
8. The pool of Mira heals the Duskbloom triggered infection.
9. The pool of Nyra can poison the pool of Mira and the Featherbough trees, but the Duskbloom is immune to it.
